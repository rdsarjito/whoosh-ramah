Three sibling Flutter `StatefulWidget`/`StatelessWidget` screens under `lib/screens/` that implement a linear passenger journey:
- `penumpang_tiket_screen.dart` (Layar 1) renders the ticket detail using `DummyData.tiket`, `HeroHeader`, `WhiteCard`, and bottom navigation; it navigates to `PenumpangPesanScreen` via two entry points (CTA button and bottom-nav 'Bantuan').
- `penumpang_pesan_screen.dart` (Layar 2) collects `JenisBantuan` + pickup point via reusable `_ChoiceBox` widgets, calls `PermintaanService.buatPermintaan` (lazily instantiated or injected through the optional `service` parameter), then `pushReplacement`s to `PenumpangStatusScreen(idPermintaan: id)`.
- `penumpang_status_screen.dart` (Layar 3) subscribes to `PermintaanService.streamPermintaan(idPermintaan)` via `StreamBuilder`, renders a hero pill, an assigned-officer card, and a 5-step timeline driven by `StatusPermintaan`; it also pushes to `KeluargaPantauScreen` and `SegeraHadirScreen`.

Dependency direction is one-way: tiket → pesan → status. Cross-cutting UI (`HeroHeader`, `HeroBackButton`, `AppColors`, `AppRadius`) lives in `../widgets` and `../theme.dart`; domain data (`DummyData`, `Permintaan`, `JenisBantuan`, `StatusPermintaan`) and persistence (`PermintaanService`) live in `../data` / `../models` / `../services`. The screens are intentionally thin — business logic stays in services, while stateful screens only hold local form state and stream subscriptions.
