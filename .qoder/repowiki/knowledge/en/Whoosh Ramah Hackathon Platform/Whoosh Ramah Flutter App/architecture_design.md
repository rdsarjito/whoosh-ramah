Three concentric scopes under `lib/` wired by Dart imports: `app_shell` (`main.dart`, `theme.dart`) initializes Firebase via `firebase_options.dart` and owns the single `MaterialApp`; `shared_layers` exposes the `Permintaan` domain model plus its `PermintaanService` (Firestore CRUD + demo fixtures) and reusable widgets; `penumpang_flows` implements passenger-facing screens that consume both the shared layers and the shell's theme. The Android native entry point (`android/app/src/main/kotlin/id/kcic/whoosh_ramah/MainActivity.kt`) is the standard Flutter-generated activity with no custom logic beyond the plugin registrant, so all orchestration lives in Dart.
