Four sub-packages with a clear dependency direction: `data/dummy.dart` is a leaf of hard-coded demo entities (`PenumpangDummy`, `PetugasDummy`, `TiketDummy`, `DummyData`) with no app dependencies; `models/permintaan.dart` defines the immutable domain types (`JenisBantuan`, `StatusPermintaan`, `RiwayatStatus`, `Permintaan`) plus Firestore serialization (`fromFirestore`/`toFirestore`) and enum-to-string round-trips (`dariValue`); `services/permintaan_service.dart` wraps the `permintaan_pendampingan` Firestore collection, exposing stream-based reads (`streamPermintaan`, `streamSemua`, `streamTerbaruPenumpang`) and mutations (`buatPermintaan`, `majukanStatus`), and injects an optional `FirebaseFirestore` for testability; `widgets/` holds presentation-only components (`bottom_nav.dart`, `pill_button.dart`, `white_card.dart`, `hero_header.dart`, etc.) that consume `AppColors`/`AppRadius` from the app theme. The service depends on both `models` and `data`; widgets depend only on the app theme — there is no cross-import between models and widgets.
